## Bountiful Botany

Adds a bountiful amount of botanical features.

### Flower Stacking :D

Small flowers can now be stacked up to four, allowing for much bushier (or flowerier) flower gardens! Flowers can now also be bonemealed to increase the stage.

### Fruits!
New fruits have been added! Harvested from the leaves of a fruit tree by right clicking. Fruit trees will spawn naturally throughout the world.

<details>
<summary>Fruits and Spawning Location</summary>
- Apple, found in forest/plains biomes
- Green Apple, rarely replaces apple trees
- Lime, found in jungles
- Lemon, found in savannas
- Orange, found in birch forests
- Cherry, found in cherry biomes
- Pear, found in forests
</details>

### Candy :3 
All fruit types have a candy, these can be put inside of a candy basket. This candy basket does not cause any lag or inventory cluttering :3
